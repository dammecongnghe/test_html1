A quick, no-fuss oz to lbs converter

If you've ever stared at a recipe that lists 28 ounces of flour while your scale reads in pounds, you know the feeling. This oz to lbs converter does the division for you, so you can get on with cooking, packing, or weighing.

It works online on any phone or computer, and it acts as a tiny converter generator too: the Copy result button hands you a tidy line like 24 oz = 1.5 lb to paste into a note, message, or spreadsheet.

How to convert ounces to pounds

  1. Enter the ounces in the box. The pounds show up underneath as you type.
  2. Pick your rounding with the decimal places menu if you need more or fewer digits.
  3. Going the other way? Press Swap to turn pounds into ounces.
  4. Copy the result to share or save it.

To do it by hand, remember that one pound is 16 ounces:

pounds = ounces ÷ 16   |   ounces = pounds × 16

One thing to watch: weight ounces vs. fluid ounces

This converter is for ounces of weight (avoirdupois ounces), the kind on a kitchen or postal scale. Fluid ounces measure volume, such as a cup of milk, and don't convert to pounds in the same way because it depends on what the liquid is. If your recipe says "fl oz," you'll want a volume conversion instead.

How many ounces are in a pound?

There are 16 ounces in one pound. To convert ounces to pounds by hand, divide the number of ounces by 16. For example, 40 oz is 2.5 lb, because 40 divided by 16 is 2.5.

Is my data private when I use this converter?
