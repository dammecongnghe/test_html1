A random food generator for the "I don't know, what do you want?" moments

Deciding what to eat can take longer than cooking it. This random food generator takes that decision off your plate. It looks through over a hundred everyday meals, snacks, and desserts from around the world and hands you one idea at a time.

It's a free random food generator you can use online on your phone or computer, whether you're planning dinner for the week, settling a debate with friends, or just bored of the same three lunches.

How to use it

  1. Pick a meal type if you have one in mind: breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack, or dessert. Leave it on Any if you're open to anything.
  2. Choose a cuisine or tick Vegetarian only to narrow the list.
  3. Press Pick my food and see what comes up.
  4. Reroll as many times as you like. The generator avoids showing the same dish twice in a row, and your recent picks stay listed below so you can go back to one.

Making the most of a random pick

Treat it as a starting point. If the generator says "Pad Thai" and you don't feel like noodles, that's useful information too. Pick again, or notice what you were hoping for.

Use filters when you're short on ideas for a specific moment. A quick snack, a vegetarian dinner for a guest, or a dessert for tonight all come out better when you narrow things down first.

Check ingredients and allergies yourself. The list covers classic versions of each dish, and recipes vary, so look at what's actually in your version before you cook or order.

Frequently asked questions

What is the random food generator?

It is a free random food generator that suggests something to eat when you can't make up your mind. Press a button and it picks a dish at random from a list of popular meals, snacks, and desserts from cuisines around the world.

How do I use the random food generator online?

Press Pick my food and a dish appears. If you want something specific, choose a meal type like breakfast, dinner, or dessert, pick a cuisine, or tick the vegetarian box first. Not feeling it? Press the button again for a new idea. Your last few picks are listed underneath so you can go back to one you liked.

Is this free random food generator really free?

Yes. It is completely free, with no account, no sign-up, and no limit on how many times you can spin for a new idea.

Can I choose the type of food I get?

Yes. You can narrow the random picks by meal type (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack, or dessert), by cuisine such as Italian, Mexican, Japanese, or Indian, and by vegetarian dishes only. The tool also tells you how many dishes match your filters.

Is my data private when I use this generator?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser. Your filter choices and picks are never sent to a server or saved, and they disappear when you close or refresh the page.
