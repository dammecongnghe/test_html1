How the conversion works

EDT stands for Eastern Daylight Time, which is UTC-4. EST is Eastern Standard Time, which is UTC-5. That makes EST exactly one hour behind EDT, so converting from EDT to EST just means subtracting one hour.

The only catch is midnight. If you convert 12:30 AM EDT, the answer is 11:30 PM EST on the day before, and the tool shows that for you. Here are a few common times for quick reference:

What is the difference between EDT and EST?

EDT is UTC-4 and EST is UTC-5, so EST is exactly one hour behind EDT. To go from EDT to EST, subtract one hour: 3:00 PM EDT is 2:00 PM EST. This EDT to EST generator does that math for you and adjusts the date when the result lands on the previous day.

Note: the EST label here always means UTC-5. In parts of the US and Canada, local clocks switch between EDT (roughly March to early November) and EST (the rest of the year).
