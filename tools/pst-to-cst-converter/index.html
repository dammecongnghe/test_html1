Good to know about PST and CST

Daylight saving. Most of the year the Pacific zone is on PDT and the Central zone is on CDT, and people still say "PST" and "CST" out of habit. Both zones switch on the same dates, so the gap stays at two hours. This tool shows the correct label for the date you pick.

"CST" can mean more than one thing. Here it means North American Central Time. The same letters are also used for China Standard Time, which is a completely different zone, so double-check if a meeting invite doesn't say where it's from.
